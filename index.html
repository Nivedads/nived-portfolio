About Me

Hey, I’m Amal Nived.

I’m a performance marketer from Calicut, Kerala. I run paid campaigns and build Shopify stores, landing pages, and tracking systems that connect ad spend to sales. Running my own e-commerce brand, Lexen, gives me firsthand experience with product offers, checkout, and order follow-up.

From the first click to the sale.

I plan campaigns around the business goal, test creative and offers, and use conversion data to decide what to change. For lead generation, I look at what happens after the form is submitted so campaign decisions can reflect lead quality.
