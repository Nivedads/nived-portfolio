Google Ads Management for Businesses

Reach people searching for your products or services

Nived Digital manages Google Ads for businesses seeking enquiries, customers and sales. Services cover Search, Performance Max and YouTube, with Shopping campaigns for businesses that sell products. Campaign selection starts with your goals, target markets and existing account performance.

Search and Shopping campaign support

Search campaign work includes keyword targeting and brand and competitor search strategy. Shopping and Performance Max work includes product feed optimization so campaign planning stays connected to the products available in your store.

Review the full path to purchase

An account review looks at bidding efficiency, campaign structure and conversion tracking alongside the landing page experience. Google Ads performance is easier to interpret when enquiry and purchase events are reviewed alongside business results.

Start with an account audit

Share your website URL, current markets and campaign goals. Existing account history, product feed issues and conversion tracking details help identify the next steps. The audit is a starting point for an agreed plan; results depend on your offer, budget and market.
