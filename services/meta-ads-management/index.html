Meta Ads Management for Ecommerce Brands

Facebook and Instagram campaigns built around your store

Meta Ads management connects your offer, creative and landing page with the people most likely to buy. Nived Digital works with ecommerce and DTC brands on prospecting, retargeting and campaign optimization across Facebook and Instagram.

What the service covers

Campaign structure and audience planning, creative hook testing, ad copy, prospecting and retargeting are coordinated with your store experience. Tracking reviews help identify gaps in purchase events before budget decisions are made.

How campaign work begins

Start with an ad account audit covering current campaigns, creative fatigue, audience overlap and post-click friction. The findings guide which tests to run first and which parts of the buying journey need attention.

What to prepare for an audit

Bring your store URL, target markets, current ad budget and campaign history. Your average order value, product margins and acquisition costs help put reported ROAS in context. Access requirements and the scope of work can be discussed during the consultation.
