Meta Ads Management for Businesses

Facebook and Instagram campaigns built around your business

Meta Ads management connects your offer, creative and landing page with people who may become your customers. Nived Digital helps businesses reach potential customers, generate enquiries and drive sales through Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

What the service covers

Campaign structure and audience planning, creative hook testing, ad copy, prospecting and retargeting are coordinated with your website and customer journey. Tracking reviews help identify gaps in enquiry and purchase events before budget decisions are made.

How campaign work begins

Start with an ad account audit covering current campaigns, creative fatigue, audience overlap and post-click friction. The findings guide which tests to run first and which parts of the buying journey need attention.

What to prepare for an audit

Bring your website URL, target markets, current ad budget and campaign history. Your goals, lead quality, sales and acquisition costs help put campaign performance in context. Access requirements and the scope of work can be discussed during the consultation.
